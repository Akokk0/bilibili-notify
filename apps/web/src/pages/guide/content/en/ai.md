AI is an optional addition to BN: live-chat summaries, commentary on content updates, weekly rankings, and AI maid chat in the lower-right corner. Push delivery works without it.

1. Open [AI](/ai), choose a provider such as an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, DeepSeek, or Alibaba Cloud Bailian, and enter an API key and model.
2. Send a message in the page's test panel to verify the connection.
3. Enable the main AI switch, then turn on the features you want, such as summaries, commentary, or chat.

The guide's AI check completes when the credentials are configured and the main AI switch is on.
